# The story

## THE ONE DISTINCTION THAT MUST NEVER COLLAPSE

**DISCOVERED in Siberia. GROWN in a laboratory outside Moscow.** Two different places, two
different events, ~2 years apart, on opposite sides of a continent. The material already existed
and was already in use when it was discovered; the discovery was of the *stone*, not of the method.
**Nothing may ever imply it is manufactured in Siberia.** The name commemorates where Stephan
Schwartz first saw it, not where it is made.

## 1. The material
Laboratory-grown crystal, its blue produced by **cobalt incorporated during hydrothermal growth** —
so the colour is intrinsic and permanent, never a surface dye, coating or irradiation treatment.
Lead with the brand name in public copy, not the technical descriptor.

## 2. The discovery — Siberia, late 1980s
**Stephan Schwartz**, American researcher and explorer, was in the USSR on a scientific mission,
invited through a Soviet Academy of Science contact into a **closed security city** — no name, only
a number, not on any map. He was taken to a **shamanic healing ceremony** in a Soviet-bloc apartment
building. At the end, a shaman produced a medicine bag made from a **tanned reindeer scrotum** and
drew out a rough shard of vivid blue crystal — described as the most powerful tool they used: it
amplified their healing gift and opened their spiritual sight (pointing to the third eye). Asked
where it came from, they became guarded, put it away, changed the subject. Schwartz inferred a
military/KGB origin on the spot.

**The name:** coined by Schwartz's late wife **Hayden** — "Siberian Blue."

## 3. Two years of silence
Schwartz was spending ~4 months a year in the USSR. For ~2 years he asked after the blue stone in
Moscow and St Petersburg. Nobody knew anything.

## 4. The contact — Moscow
On an unannounced last-minute trip he checked into the **Rossiya Hotel**, which he had never used
before. The phone in his room was **already ringing** as he walked in. A Russian voice said his
name, asked if he was still interested in the blue, and said: *"You will be at the place of public
execution tomorrow at eight hundred and you will be banded. It will be made clear."* — the historic
execution spot in **Red Square**: ominous phrasing, entirely public place.

## 5. The drive
08:00, met by a **KGB colonel in full regalia**, into a **ZIL limousine** with green cloth curtains
drawn. **Roughly three hours**, in near-total silence. Through a gap in the curtain: open rural
countryside that reminded him of Minnesota.

## 6. The laboratory — outside Moscow
From the outside, a **collective dairy farm** with wagons — and **two armed guards** on the gate.
Inside: *"we're in the 21st century… clearly some sort of laboratory, there are people in spacesuits,
ultra sanitary working conditions."* Schwartz read the farm as a deliberate facade for American spy
satellites. The facility head is named only as **Boris**, who described it as an inorganic crystal
laboratory manufacturing for government agencies: *"We take natural stones and we transform them."*

**The facility has never been identified.** No institute, programme or scientist name exists in any
vault source. **Never speculate on its identity.** Its only fixed coordinates: ~3 hours by car from
Moscow, countryside, disguised as a dairy farm.

## 7. How it was grown
Natural quartz manipulated molecularly; **seed crystals lowered into titanium autoclaves**, sealed
and heated, the crystal growing outward from the seed in hot liquid.
- **~2 months per crystal** (plus ~2 weeks cooling, per the primary transcript).
- **300–400°C, under pressure.**

> 🔴 **The 2500°C figure is WRONG.** It appears on the old website, in Schwartz's own transcript and
> in a 2019 telling — **Stephan misspoke.** Silica melts ~1713°C and decomposes well below 2500°C;
> quartz would vaporise. **Never publish it.**
> 🔴 **Never publish a pressure figure.** The widely-repeated ~1,500 atmospheres has **no primary
> source anywhere in the vault**. §7 says "under pressure", unquantified. Say exactly that.

## 8. What it was built for
Boris, directly: *"We make it into laser discs to disable your spy satellites and for nuclear
torpedo triggers."* Non-lethal defensive laser optics — blinding sensors, not people. The military
requirement for **laser-grade optical purity** is why the original material is irreplaceable:
nothing produced since matches it.

**Schwartz's own framing, which OSB carries forward rather than replacing:**
*"Oh, then this is a kind of swords into plowshares thing. That's great, thank you. I will be sure
and tell people that."* His creative intention was to carve it into dolphins — the Peace Dolphin.

## 9. The purchase
Asked how much he would take: *"A thousand kilos."* A formal negotiated purchase in hard currency,
with Schwartz personally examining, initialling and numbering the material. On arrival in Los
Angeles **US customs impounded it** (no airport named in the source).

## 10. The end of supply
The Soviet Union dissolved **Christmas Day 1991**; the facility ceased to exist roughly three months
later. **No more will ever be made.** The stock then sat **~30 years in sealed storage**, still in
its original **metal-banded wooden shipping crates**, with the **original Russian laboratory
stickers** intact. Adrian was the first person to break those seals.
