Multimodal input, 262K native context. **[CONFIRMED the engine exists; speeds are community self-reports; repo attribution differs between sources (HN: Niko1221, search: SSatoya) — treat the codebase as unvetted]** — [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49953495), [GitHub](https://github.com/SSatoya/Strata), [VRAM Calculator](https://vramcalculator.com/strata-qwen3-8-flash-next/), [MarkTechPost on the model](https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/08/26/alibabas-qwen-team-releases-qwen3-8-flash-next-a-125b-multimodal-moe-with-6b-active-parameters-previewing-the-qwen4-architecture/)
