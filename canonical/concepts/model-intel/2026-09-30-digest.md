- **OpenAI GPT-6.1 Sol, released 29 Sep at DevDay.** $2 in / $10 out per M, $0.10 cached input, which is exactly one fifth of GPT-6 Astra's $10 / $50. It is in ChatGPT Work and Codex for Plus, Pro, Business, Enterprise and Edu, and on the API as `gpt-6.1-sol`. OpenAI says it is near-Astra on coding, computer use and professional workflows. It also introduced an **Ultrafast** inference tier: up to 8x faster in Codex, 6x on the API, up to 300 tokens per second. Why it matters: it lowers the price of the strongest OpenAI model that is not Astra. "Near-Astra" is OpenAI's claim, and the [09-28 digest](2026-09-28-digest.md) recorded independent scoring of the earlier GPT-6 Sol as a price cut, not an intelligence gain. [CONFIRMED — [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/introducing-gpt-6-1-sol/), [VentureBeat](https://venturebeat.com/technology/openais-gpt-6-1-sol-offers-astra-like-performance-at-1-5th-price-a-new-ultrafast-tier-clocks-at-300-tokens-per-second), [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/29/openai-launches-gpt-6-1-sol-says-it-nearly-matches-gpt-6-astra-and-costs-less/)]
